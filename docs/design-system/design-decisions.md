# Design Decision Log

## Decision: Why Purple is Primary

- Reason: Purple conveys calm premium focus and aligns with audio-first, modern product experiences.
- Impact: Primary actions, active states, and selection states use a consistent semantic anchor.
- Future Considerations: If the brand evolves, this token can be rebalanced without changing semantic usage.

## Decision: Why Green is Reserved for Playback

- Reason: Green is associated with play, success, and progress, making it intuitive for listening workflows.
- Impact: Playback and success states remain visually consistent and easy to learn.
- Future Considerations: Keep green limited to playback/success and avoid using it for general CTAs.

## Decision: Why Olive is Metadata Only

- Reason: Olive is less dominant and works well for secondary labels and metadata without competing with primary actions.
- Impact: Supporting content remains readable while primary interactions stay prominent.
- Future Considerations: Reserve olive for non-critical semantic use only.

## Decision: Why Radius MD is Default

- Reason: Medium radius feels modern, soft, and approachable while remaining structured.
- Impact: Cards, inputs, and buttons share a predictable visual rhythm.
- Future Considerations: Keep radius MD as baseline and use larger radii only for richer surfaces such as player and dialog.

## Decision: Why Motion Stays Subtle

- Reason: The product should feel calm and focused rather than playful or noisy.
- Impact: Interaction feedback remains noticeable without being distracting.
- Future Considerations: If future features require stronger feedback, increase intensity only in targeted cases.
