# Brand Principles

## Brand Personality

Castaminofen should feel premium, calm, intelligent, modern, and audio-first. The experience should feel polished without being flashy, and it should make listening feel effortless from the first interaction.

## Core Principles

- Minimal surfaces with generous spacing.
- Calm motion and restrained elevation.
- Clear hierarchy built around readable typography and intentional contrast.
- Audio-first emphasis through purple and green accents used with purpose.
- Consistency over novelty; shared tokens should drive UI decisions.

## Visual Tone

- Premium and focused rather than playful.
- Soft and understated rather than loud.
- Confident and professional rather than experimental.
